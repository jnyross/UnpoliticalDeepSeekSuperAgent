Party offers: a 2024 snapshot

This is history, not a list of current plans. The Commons roster is as of 2024-07-09. The offers are from the parties' 2024 general election manifestos. We do not say these plans are now law, funded or still party policy.

Each party gets one selected tax, benefit or funding offer, the same fields and the same word limits. Parties are in name order. These are examples, not the full plans or a ranking. We use no party colours or scores.

How to read this page

The offer is a short summary of a party claim. The quote is the party's own text, not our view. We do not assess its impact. Our selection is qualitative: it does not report a cash cost, yield or impact estimate. A missing cost is not zero. “Unavailable here” means not supplied for this selected offer in this dataset, not that none exists elsewhere.

We use the same rule for all parties: one direct tax, benefit or public funding pledge with a short source quote. We choose qualitative passages that need no cash cost or impact estimate. This choice does not show which policy matters most to a party. Each source is a party-issued manifesto. Some retained PDFs come from the British Manifesto Archive, not the party's server; these are labelled. Dates are stated to year precision where a full date has not been checked. PDF page numbers count from the first page, including the cover. Text quotes retain the words, with line breaks removed.

The limits, in words, are: offer 45; quote 85; scope and baseline 30 each; year and status 25 each; costing and assessment 30 each. Party names and source locators have a 15-word limit each. Source titles have a 35-word limit; host labels 30 words; source URLs 2,000 characters. The checks apply these limits to every party.

The Speaker is listed on their own (1 seat), not as a party offer. Sinn Féin is included on the same basis as all other parties in the official Commons roster; taking seats is not our coverage rule.
