View shown: Inflation-adjusted spending (£ billion, 2025-26 prices). This covers 2003-04 to 2025-26. Fiscal years run from April to March. All figures are recorded spending (outturn). They may still change.

Cash spendingInflation-adjusted spendingShare of the economy

Cash is the amount spent at the time. Real spending allows for price changes. It uses 2025-26 prices and ONS GDP deflators released 30 June 2026. Share of the economy means spending as a share of UK GDP. This uses ONS GDP published 30 June 2026. All three views come from the Treasury source. They use the same workbook and years. They cover the same UK area and the same types of spending. We do not work out new values.

Source: Public Expenditure Statistical Analyses 2026, Chapter 4, HM Treasury. Published 2026-07-16; source page updated 2026-07-16. No next release date is announced in the retained release metadata. This history is from PESA Chapter 4, July 2026. The latest overview uses a separate workbook: Chapter 5 of the July 2026 spending statistics. Its source page was updated later. We keep a source record for each workbook. We do not mix their values. The sources round to different units. This history uses £0.1 billion. The overview uses £ million. Each subcategory is part of its parent area. Do not add it twice. Spending on services and Total Managed Expenditure (TME) are different totals. The source rounds all values to one decimal place.
