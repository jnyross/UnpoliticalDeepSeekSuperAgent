23 full fiscal years, 2003-04 to 2025-26. These are outturn figures: recorded spending that can still be revised. A fiscal year runs from April to March.

See the spending charts: cash, inflation-adjusted and GDP share. Switch views beside each chart; no JavaScript is needed.

Each table shows three views side by side: cash, real spending at 2025-26 prices and share of GDP. GDP measures the size of the economy. Real figures use ONS GDP deflators released 30 June 2026. GDP shares use ONS GDP published 30 June 2026. We use the Treasury’s published views, not a new calculation.

This is the July 2026 PESA Chapter 4 history. The latest overview uses the separate July 2026 spending-statistics Chapter 5 workbook, whose source page was later updated. We keep both source manifests and do not splice values across workbooks. Published precision differs: this history is rounded to £0.1 billion, the overview to £ million. The latest overview covers 2025-26. Its figures are not appended to these tables.

This retained functional series starts in 2003-04. For TME only, Table 4.1 also supplies the 1985-86 and 1995-96 snapshots. Other earlier fixed-decade entries are unavailable here; this is not a claim that no other official historical data exists. Subcategories are included within their parent area: do not add them again. Figures are published rounded to one decimal place; differences in sums may reflect rounding.

TES (spending on services) plus accounting adjustments gives TME (Total Managed Expenditure), subject to rounding. These are different scopes.

  • (1) This table uses the UN COFOG definition of Defence spending. Government commitments on NATO qualifying defence spending are evaluated using NATO definitions. The two are different and not comparable. NATO defence figures, for example, include pensions whereas in COFOG these are included in Social Protection. Details and figures on NATO defence expenditure can be found at the following link: (4_2!A28)
  • http://www.nato.int/cps/on/natohq/topics_49198.htm (4_2!A29)
  • (2) Transactions in 2008-09 onwards have been affected by financial sector interventions. Increases in spending from 2020-21 have been affected by measures taken in response to Covid-19 and increases in the cost of living. (4_2!A30)
  • (3) Following implementation of ESA2010, Network Rail is now classified to Central Government. Consequently Total Managed Expenditure (TME) includes Network Rail spending in all years shown, however the actual expenditure of Network Rail only appears in the Department for Transport budget from 2015-16. The functional analysis shown includes Network Rail spending from 2015-16 and is therefore only included within ‘Total Expenditure on services’ from that year. (4_2!A31)
  • (4) From 2011-12 onwards, the transport function includes expenditure relating to the local government part of the TfL subsidiary, ‘Transport Trading Limited’ in order to align with Office for National Statistics treatment. (4_2!A32)
  • (5) From 2011-12 onwards the 'grant-equivalent element of student loans' is no longer part of the TES framework and has therefore been removed from the Education function. Therefore figures are not directly comparable between 2010-11 and 2011-12. A full explanation of this decision can be found in PESA 2016 Annex E. (4_2!A33)
  • (6) From 2010-11 onwards VAT-based payments are no longer deducted from EU transactions thus bringing these totals in-line with ESA10. (4_2!A34)
  • (7) This excludes the temporary effects of banks being classified to the public sector. (4_2!A35)
