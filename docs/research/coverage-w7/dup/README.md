# DUP — verified already-approved childcare programme, not a new pledge cost

The current manifesto's PDF page 21 highlights a £25m early-learning and childcare package already announced by the DUP Education Minister. Its next paragraph separately introduces **new conditional election commitments** for workplace nurseries and capital grants. The £25m must not be attached to those unpriced promises.

A normal public request to the [Department of Education announcement](https://www.education-ni.gov.uk/news/education-minister-announces-ps25million-package-measures-early-learning-and-childcare-northern-ireland) returned **200**. [Retained raw HTML](de-childcare-2024.raw.html.txt), [deterministic text](de-childcare-2024.txt#L37-L43) and [request metadata](requests.json) establish:

- Published **23 May 2024**.
- **£25m for 2024/25**, supporting children, parents and providers with early learning and childcare.
- **Executive approval**, explicitly stated in the body.
- Prospective measures and a longer-term strategy; not merely a past record of delivered spending.
- No claim here of September implementation, recurring annual funding, an additional Westminster allocation, a known price basis, or a new party election spending commitment.

## Product decision

Select the manifesto-highlighted already-approved programme as a useful verified funding example. Explicitly distinguish it in **Offer, Scope, Baseline, Year, Status and Costing**. The changed Status is factual, not preferential treatment: every card retains the same fields, word limits, order and neutral styling. A minister's approval is **not an independent assessment**. The independent-assessment count stays nine, with DUP, Sinn Féin, SDLP and TUV still unmatched.

Keep the prospective nursery/grant residue visible. Sinn Féin's selected offer remains uncosted. Do not describe this update as obtaining a cost for the whole DUP manifesto, its new conditional promises, or Coverage L4.
