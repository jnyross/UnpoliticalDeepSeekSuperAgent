## Exact limited quotations and model meaning

On **physical PDF5 / printed3**, key finding5 says:

> The triple lock also generates considerable uncertainty for the future level of public spending on the state pension.

The same finding estimates additional annual spending in **2050** above **earnings indexation**, with an **80%** interval, between **£5bn and £45bn** in the source's **“today’s terms”**, accounting for the growing pensioner population. The original body repeats this on **physical PDF12 / printed10**. These are conditional long-run model outcomes, not a 2024 manifesto price tag or a point estimate. Do not silently strengthen the price-basis description to a verified named base year.

The simulation draws on inflation/earnings history for1993–2023 and is sensitive to whether that history represents future volatility. The DUP quotation does not itself promise a policy horizon through2050. Any future use must say **if the rule were continued to2050**, distinguish the earnings-only counterfactual from current-policy spending, identify the model vintage, and not call the estimate a five-year party costing or an NI allocation. Other pension promises on the same manifesto page are not assessed by this passage.
