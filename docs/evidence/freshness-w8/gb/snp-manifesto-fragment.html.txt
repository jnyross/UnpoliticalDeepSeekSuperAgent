            <p class="p1">We are firmly focused on what matters most to the people of Scotland &#8211; tackling the cost of living crisis, improving the performance of our NHS and taking our enormous energy wealth into our own hands.</p>
<p class="p1">On May 7th choose a Scottish Government that works for Scotland. Choose an SNP government that, day in and day out, is always on Scotland’s side.</p>
<p class="p1">Your first SNP vote at this election will deliver an SNP Government. It delivers a better NHS, GP walk-in clinics, help with the cost of living, the first-time buyers’ deposit, a growing economy, and a massive increase in childcare that leaves no one behind.</p>
<p class="p1">Your second SNP vote delivers independence. It secures a majority that will lay the path to a referendum. A referendum that we will win.</p>
<p class="p1"><strong>On May 7th choose a Scottish Government that works for Scotland. Choose an SNP government that is always on Scotland’s side.</strong></p>
