We are standing up for you
As the largest party on the island, Sinn Féin is leading positive change in communities and working to deliver a new and united Ireland.
In Dublin, Belfast, London, Brussels, and further afield, Sinn Féin representatives are your voice where it matters.
Policies
Housing – Tithíocht
We will make housing affordable and bring homeownership back into reach for working people.
Health – Sláinte
Our plan will transform healthcare and ensure you get the care you need when you need it.
€10 A Day Childcare – Cúram Leanaí €10 an Lae
Our plan will make Ireland a better place to raise a family and build a future.
Scrap the USC for average workers – Faigh réidh leis an Mhuirear Sóisialta Uilíoch don mheánoibrí
Sinn Féin will scrap the USC for ALL workers on the first €45,000 you earn.
Abolish the means test for carers – Cuir deireadh leis an dtástáil acmhainne don liúntas cúramóra
A Sinn Féin led Government will abolish the means test for carer’s allowance.
Planning for Irish Unity – Pleanáil ar son Aontú na hÉireann
We will ensure Irish Unity is at the heart of our vision for the future and will begin planning and preparing for reunification.
Tackling government waste – Dul i ngleic le cur amú airgid an rialtais
Sinn Féin will end the constant waste of public money and ensure accountability and reform.
Investing in communities – Ag Infheistiú i bpobail
We will invest €1 billion of the Apple Tax money to transform communities left behind by Fianna Fáil/Fine Gael for decades.
